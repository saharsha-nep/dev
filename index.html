Our Story

While our team was strolling the streets of Kathmandu, we noticed that most apartment complexes or flats lacked greenery. They lacked "Life". Seeing such a sight, we interviewed a few of the residents, just to find out that they DID want plants, just couldn't figure out how to grow them inside such small spaces. After hearing this, we couldn't just stay still and immediately got to work on starting UrbanSprout, a way to bring nature into the city life.
