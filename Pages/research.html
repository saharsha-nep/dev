Research

Navigation Bar

Our website uses a simple horizontal navigation bar that contains only essential pages such as Home, Products, Blog, Research, About, and Login. This reduces unnecessary complexity and allows users to access content quickly.

In comparison, platforms such as Daraz use multi-level navigation systems with dropdown categories, promotional banners, and filtering options. While this supports a large e-commerce system, it increases visual complexity and may overwhelm first-time users.

Our design improves usability by reducing cognitive load and ensuring faster navigation between pages.

Product Page

The product page is designed using a structured table layout with consistent image sizes, clear pricing, and minimal visual distractions. This ensures that users can focus directly on product information without being overloaded by unnecessary elements.

In comparison, Daraz includes ratings, advertisements, filters, and recommendations alongside product listings. While this improves functionality for large-scale shopping, it can reduce clarity for users looking for simple browsing.

Our approach prioritizes simplicity, readability, and faster decision-making for users interested in urban gardening products.

Sign-in Page

The sign-in page is designed with minimal input fields to ensure a fast and distraction-free login experience. Users only need to enter email and password to proceed.

In contrast, platforms such as Facebook include multiple login options, password recovery features, and additional interface elements. While these features improve flexibility, they increase complexity for a simple login task.

Our version improves efficiency by focusing only on essential authentication elements.

Visual Design and Layout

The website maintains a consistent background image and soft color palette to ensure visual harmony across all pages. Spacing and alignment are kept uniform to improve readability and user experience.

Compared to platforms like HamroBazar, which often display dense layouts and uneven spacing due to high content volume, our design maintains a cleaner structure.

This improves accessibility and ensures that users can easily scan and understand content.

Usability and Simplicity

Overall, the website prioritizes simplicity over feature-heavy design. This ensures faster navigation, easier understanding, and better usability for first-time users.

Compared to large e-commerce platforms like Daraz, which focus on maximizing features and content density, our design focuses on clarity and essential functionality.

This makes the website more suitable for beginners and aligns with the goal of promoting simple urban gardening solutions.
