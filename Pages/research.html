Research

Navigation Bar

Inspiration was taken from W3School. Instead of cluttering our navbar like W3School, we chose to make it minimalistic, removing the search bar as well as the high amount of hyperlinks. Color was also changed to a more warm tone to make it feel more comfortable.

Product Page

While we did take inspiration from Daraz's product page, ours is far cleaner. Daraz's has a very cluttered page, filled with random products that dont fit in tandem with one another. However, our products page is sectioned properly, small descriptions are added to the products, and overall its nicer to look at.

Product Description Page

Our product description page is far more minimal compared to the page HamroBazar provides. Our description is easy to look at, buying is easy, reviews are easy to read. However HamroBazar has a very haphazard information filled page, overwhelming the user. There is no clear indication of how to buy the product and description is usually not filled with item details.

Home Page

While inspiration was certainly taken from TheSill, we decided to make our design less selling based and more so an experience so users feel more at peace, just how urban gardening is meant to be. We talk about what we provide, and share our story, while TheSill shows products from the get go. However, the idea of some sort of greenery on the home page was derived from how TheSill shows beautiful flower products on their home page as well, which is why we have the flowery vintage background throughout our website.

Login Page

Our Login page took inspiration from the classic Google sign in page, but instead of the wide look, we opted for a bordered rectangle in the middle, nice and simple. With the classic background that is a common theme throughout the website, our login page is a minimalistic version of the Google sign in page. With the simpler design, it is far easier to navigate around.
